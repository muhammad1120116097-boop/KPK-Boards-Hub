What can you find on a board website?

Each board manages its own examinations and student services. Open your board's official portal for the latest notices and online facilities.

  • Matric (SSC) and Intermediate (HSSC) results
  • Exam date sheets and schedules
  • Roll number slips and exam notices
  • Online admission, registration and enrollment
  • Rechecking, retotalling and certificate information

Note: KP Board Hub is an independent directory, not a government website. Board links open external official portals. Board emblems are sourced from board websites or identifiable board publications; if a source changes, use the official board link to verify. Results, schedules and services are controlled by each board. Always confirm important information on the official board website.
